# Homepage (/)

Scope: the site homepage (`index.md`, new `home` sections). Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: employers first, research peers second. Action: open the CV or the publications; contact is one step away. Proof: the instrument he built, real publications, prize-winning poster. Lab notes stay off the homepage (owner's call, 2026-10-19: too busy there). Constraints: CV identity (PRODUCT.md), static Jekyll, self-hosted assets, compressed images.

Memorable moment: the scanning Fabry-Pérot interferometer assembles itself as you scroll.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage shows the instrument, not a stock hero. A visitor scrolls and the exploded scanning Fabry-Pérot interferometer closes into one part while the chain of his work (optics, experiments, modelling, software) fills in beneath it. It refuses the photo-hero-with-tagline-and-button and the row of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: CV identity. Paper #f8f7f7 ground, ink #231f20, yellow #fdcb36 only as fills, markers and underlines, charcoal #2c2d30 for the closing band. Antonio uppercase at poster scale for the name and section heads; Alegreya for prose. Hairline ink rules, a yellow bar as the progress and active marker. No cards, no shadows, no icon tiles.

STORY: Who he is and what he does (name, role, PhD) → he built this instrument, end to end → proof: selected publications, posters → CV and contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim yellow masthead. Left 40%: "MADS NIBE LARSEN" in Antonio caps around 7–8vw, role line in Alegreya 24px, actions "Read the CV" (yellow fill) and "Publications" (ink outline). Right 60%, bleeding off the edge: the exploded interferometer render on paper. Bottom: a full-width ink rule with four chain steps; a yellow bar on the rule tracks scroll progress.

FORM: Assembly scroll, position 3 on the ordered list (dealt lead). seed:9fa2fe9eaffc7334. Signature interaction: sticky stage, scroll scrubs a canvas image sequence (render frames keyed to alpha) from exploded to assembled; chain steps light in sequence; reduced motion shows the still exploded render with all steps visible.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
