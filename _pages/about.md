I am an engineer with a PhD in the development and application of hyperspectral imaging systems for the long-wave infrared (LWIR). I work across the full stack of these systems, from optics and experimental work through physical modeling and inverse problems to the software that processes the data.

I also implement AI and machine learning solutions and build data processing pipelines that turn large datasets into usable results. I have a keen eye for detail and enjoy presenting science in a clear and easily digestible way.
